# Where the Roots Dance - custom gap-fill prototypes

These are audition prototypes generated on 2026-08-29. They are not final mastered game assets.

The helmet breathing and pollution ambience layers were synthesized locally from noise, periodic tones and amplitude envelopes.

Plant and Boss prototypes are derived from these CC0 sources:

- Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
- Kenney RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- 80 CC0 RPG SFX by rubberduck: https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx

Source license: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0)

License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Processing included resampling/pitch adjustment, filtering, envelopes, fades and layered mixing. The periodic pollution layers were constructed for seamless mathematical loop boundaries. Loop behavior must still be checked inside Unity after compression and import.
