## About Unity 6 releases

With the Unity 6 family of releases, we’ve changed how we release the Unity Engine to improve performance and stability, while making it easier and less disruptive for you to upgrade. The Unity 6.3 LTS release includes major improvements in functionality and quality. This will be followed by regular Update releases which will deliver important bug fixes, platform support, and new features.

## Long-term support (LTS)

Released once a year

Supported for two years

Additional year of support for Unity Enterprise and Unity Industry users

Regular patches for bug fixes and critical platform updates

##### Is the LTS release right for me?

LTS releases are recommended for live service games and creators who are about to lock in production on a specific version of Unity.  
  
Unity 6.3 LTS is supported until December 2027, while Unity 6.0 LTS is supported through October 2026.

## Supported

Multiple Update releases a year

Supported until the next release is published

Same level of support as LTS with weekly patches

Fully production-ready with same QA testing as LTS

##### Is the Update release right for me?

Update releases are the preferred choice for new and mid-cycle productions as they contain the latest features, platform support, and performance improvements.

## Frequently asked questions

### <span class="mango-text-heading-3xs pr-4 text-left">What version of Unity 6 do you recommend for my project?</span>

LTS releases are recommended for live service games and creators who are about to lock in production on a specific version of Unity.

Update releases are recommended for new and mid-cycle productions as they contain the latest features, platform support, and performance improvements.

### <span class="mango-text-heading-3xs pr-4 text-left">How do I get access to your alpha and beta releases of Unity?</span>

Our alpha and beta releases are open to anyone, so no signup is required. Get started by downloading them from the [Unity Hub.](https://unity.com/download) Because there may be feature stability issues with early test versions, we do not recommend them for projects in production, and we highly recommend that you back up any project before opening it with an alpha or beta release.

### <span class="mango-text-heading-3xs pr-4 text-left">How are Unity 6 Update releases different from Tech Streams?</span>

All Update releases undergo the same rigorous quality assurance and stability testing as our LTS releases.

Update releases are production-ready and get the same level of support as an LTS, unlike previous Tech Stream releases, which were primarily for early testing of new features.

### <span class="mango-text-heading-3xs pr-4 text-left">Why should I use a Unity 6 Update release instead of LTS?</span>

Update releases are recommended for new and mid-cycle productions as they contain the latest features, platform support, and performance improvements. Update releases are production-ready and get the same QA testing and level of support as an LTS.

We have prioritized easier upgrades between Unity 6 releases so you can confidently upgrade to the latest Update and benefit from improved performance, stability, and support.
