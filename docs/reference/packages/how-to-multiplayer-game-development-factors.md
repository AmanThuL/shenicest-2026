Quickly experiment with multiplayer solutions

With [Multiplayer Center](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/multiplayer/multiplayer-center) and Widgets, you get a curated list of multiplayer features and services to implement from a pitchable prototype through development.

Validate multiplayer gameplay in-Editor

[Multiplayer Play Mode](https://docs-multiplayer.unity3d.com/mppm/current/about/index.html) and Play Mode Scenarios provide validation of your gameplay early and often, without leaving the Editor.

Ship resilient, client-hosted games

Build client-hosted, cost-efficient, and resilient games using Unity’s Netcode packages along with [Distributed Authority](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.netcode.gameobjects@latest?subfolder=/manual/terms-concepts/distributed-authority.html) for Netcode for GameObjects.

Connect players together effortlessly

The [Multiplayer Services](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/mps-sdk) package along with additional services provide everything needed to connect the right players together before, during and after the game.
